## Long bio (About Me — 3 short paragraphs)

**Intro — who & where.**
I'm gromb, a jazz dance instructor based in San Francisco. I teach **Blues** and **Balboa**, two flavors of partner dance born from the jazz and swing era, and I love meeting dancers wherever the music takes us — classes, social floors, weekend events, and festivals.

**Journey — how I got here.**
My own dance journey started with **Lindy Hop in 2016**, before the Blues floor called me in **2018** and Balboa joined the mix in **2022** (see the Dance Lineage section for the full timeline). My home scenes along the way — Oakland, DC, Triangle NC, and now SF — each shaped how I hear and move to the music, and eventually turned into the call to teach.

**Philosophy — what I teach for.**
I believe great social dance is a conversation. My classes focus on clear connection, musicality, and — above all — having fun on the dance floor. Whether you're brand new or deep in your dance journey, my goal is to make every lesson a place where you feel welcome to grow.
