About Me

I'm gromb, a jazz dance instructor based in San Francisco. I teach Blues and Balboa — two partner dances born from the jazz and swing era — and I love meeting dancers wherever the music takes us: classes, social floors, weekend events, and festivals.

My dance journey began with Lindy Hop in 2016, moved onto the Blues floor in 2018, and welcomed Balboa in 2022. Each style taught me something new about connection, rhythm, and listening — and it didn't take long before sharing that with students became the best part of the path (see the Dance Lineage section for the full timeline).

I believe great social dance is a conversation. My classes focus on clear connection, musicality, and — above all — having fun on the dance floor. Whether you're brand new or deep in your dance journey, my goal is to make every lesson a place where you feel welcome to grow.
